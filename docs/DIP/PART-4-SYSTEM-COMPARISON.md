## Conclusions from the comparison

1. **Where existing platforms are stronger:** mature commercial systems (Cropwise, EOSDA, AGRIVI, AgroSignal) provide satellite vegetation indices (NDVI) and live machinery/sensor telemetry. EGIN deliberately excludes satellite analytics and live telemetry from its current scope rather than attempting to duplicate heavyweight agronomic platforms.
2. **EGIN's specific niche:** none of the global platforms provide built-in plot lookup by a **Kazakhstan 11–12-digit cadastral number** against the public EGKN map; they require manual polygon drawing or uploading pre-prepared GIS files (Shapefile/GeoJSON). EGIN combines instant Kazakh cadastral boundary lookup, coordinate weather, offline field records with media, read-only 1C OData import, and passwordless passkey sign-in in a single cross-platform application (Capacitor for Android/iOS + Web PWA).
3. **Low entry barrier:** competing full-featured systems require sales onboarding or per-hectare subscriptions (Cropwise, AGRIVI, AgroSignal), whereas EGIN opens immediately in a mobile browser as a PWA and packages into Android/iOS apps via Capacitor without maintaining separate platform-specific codebases [1]–[3].
